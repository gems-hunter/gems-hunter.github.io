Natural Labradorite

Labradorite is a feldspar mineral known for "labradorescence": as the stone turns, flashes of blue, green, gold or orange sweep across its surface. It is named after Labrador in Canada, where it was first described. Every stone flashes differently, so no two pieces are alike. There are 27 Labradorite pieces in the shop right now, each one of a kind.
