Natural Agate

Agate is a form of chalcedony, a microcrystalline quartz, known for concentric bands formed over millions of years. Gemsland has agate slices, geodes and ring boxes carved from agate: every slice has its own pattern, lovely to display or to give. There are 14 Agate pieces in the shop right now, each one of a kind.
