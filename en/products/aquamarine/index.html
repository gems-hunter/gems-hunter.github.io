Natural Aquamarine

Aquamarine is a variety of the mineral beryl, coloured a pale sea blue by traces of iron. Its name comes from the Latin "aqua marina" (sea water), and it is the birthstone for March. At Gemsland, aquamarine is usually kept as a raw crystal wand, then fitted with a silver bail, wrapped in silver wire or set in a macramé net. There are 66 Aquamarine pieces in the shop right now, each one of a kind.
