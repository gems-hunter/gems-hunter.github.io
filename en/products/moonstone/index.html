Natural Moonstone

Moonstone is a feldspar with a soft glow that floats across its surface, called "adularescence", like moonlight on water. The most loved stones show a strong blue sheen over an almost clear body. Moonstone is one of the birthstones for June; it is fairly soft, so it is best kept away from knocks. There are 24 Moonstone pieces in the shop right now, each one of a kind.
