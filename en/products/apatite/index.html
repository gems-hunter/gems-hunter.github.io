Natural Apatite

Apatite is a phosphate mineral known for its vivid blue-green. The name comes from the Greek "apatao" (to deceive), as it was often mistaken for other minerals. Apatite is fairly soft, so it suits pendants better than rings. There are 3 Apatite pieces in the shop right now, each one of a kind.
