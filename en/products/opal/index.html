Natural Opal

Opal is a hydrated form of silica that shows "play of colour": flashes of red, orange, green and blue that shift as you move it. Gemsland carries Ethiopian and black opals, each with its own fire. Opal is the birthstone for October; it is delicate, so avoid knocks and sudden temperature changes. There are 16 Opal pieces in the shop right now, each one of a kind.
