From the earth to your hands

i.Finding the stone

We choose each natural stone by hand and keep only the ones with the most beautiful shape and colour.

ii.Made by hand

Each stone gets a bail, a silver wire wrap or a macramé net, following its own shape.

iii.On its way to you

Your piece is packed in a straw-paper box and sent off. Pay by bank transfer, or cash on delivery with a 50% deposit.
